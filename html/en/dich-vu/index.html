Precision metal machining from your drawings

We take on both single parts and production batches: cutting sheet metal from DXF/DWG files, bending, punching, structural welding and machined parts. Every stage is checked against the original drawing, with dimensions verified before moving to the next step.

Common materials include CT3, SS400 steel, galvanised sheet, 201/304 stainless steel and aluminium. If the material hasn't been decided, our technical team advises on the best balance of durability, weight and cost before quoting.

For orders that need a surface finish, welded parts are ground clean and sent straight to surface treatment and powder coating within the same workshop — no outsourcing, which shortens lead time and avoids scratching during transport.

ADDITIONAL SERVICES

Material & drawing consulting

We review dimensions and tolerances and propose a cost-effective machining plan before the order is confirmed.

Assembly

Parts are assembled into complete units after coating, ready to install as soon as they arrive.

Packing & protection

Coating is wrapped to prevent scratches, and crates are labelled by quantity and marking as the customer requires.

On-site delivery

Delivery within Binh Duong, Ho Chi Minh City and Dong Nai on an agreed schedule.

Refinishing & repainting

We strip old coating, treat the surface and reapply a fresh finish on used frames, cabinets and parts.

PROCESS & CAPABILITY

POWDER SPRAYING: electrostatic gun, even coverage on edges and inner surfaces

CURING: oven-cured for a hard, well-bonded coating

COLOUR: RAL colour chart or a customer-supplied sample

FINISH: gloss, matte, wrinkle, textured

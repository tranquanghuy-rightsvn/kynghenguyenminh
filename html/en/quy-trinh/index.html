PRODUCTION PROCESS

FROM DRAWING TO FINISHED PART

Eight steps, all within the same workshop

A customer's part goes from receiving the drawing to the packed crate without ever leaving the workshop. That lets us control dimensions at every stage, reduce the risk of scratches from moving between sites, and shorten delivery time.

For repeat orders, the jigs and coating specs from the first batch are kept on file so later batches come out exactly the same.

PRODUCTION STEPS

01 · Drawing intake & quote

We receive the drawing, sample or reference photo. Our technical team reviews dimensions and tolerances, selects materials and sends a quote with an estimated timeline.

02 · Material prep & blanking

Materials are issued to the exact type and thickness specified, with blanking planned to minimise sheet waste.

03 · CNC / plasma cutting

Cut from DXF, DWG files. Clean cut lines with minimal burr and heat distortion.

04 · Bending – punching – forming

Sheet bending and hole punching to jig. The first part of each batch is measured and checked before the full run.

05 · Welding & weld finishing

MIG/TIG/stick welding depending on material, followed by grinding and cleaning the welds.

06 · Surface treatment

Degreasing, derusting and phosphating — the step that determines how well the coating adheres.

07 · Powder coating & curing

Electrostatic powder spraying, then oven-cured for a hard, well-bonded coating.

08 · Inspection – packing – delivery

Colour, thickness and adhesion of the coating are checked, quantities counted, parts wrapped and delivered on-site.

QUALITY CONTROL

We don't push all inspection to the end of the line. After every stage, the part is checked against the drawing before moving on — an issue caught early is fixed quickly, without dragging down the whole batch.

Before delivery, the coating is checked for colour, thickness and adhesion; quantities are recounted against the delivery note and wrapped to prevent scratches.

If something is off on our side, we redo the part — please let us know with photos as soon as possible after receiving the shipment.

Checks performed at every stage

Before machining

  • Check the drawing and confirm tolerances

  • Inspect incoming materials

  • Approve the blanking plan

During machining

  • Measure dimensions after cutting and bending

  • Check welds and squareness

  • Clean the surface before coating

Before delivery

  • Check coating colour and thickness

  • Test coating adhesion

  • Count quantities, pack

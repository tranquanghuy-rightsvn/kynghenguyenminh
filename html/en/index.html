Nguyen Minh Engineering — one-stop machining and powder coating workshop

Nguyen Minh Engineering Co., Ltd. provides metal machining and powder coating for factories, production workshops and contractors in the Binh Duong area. From sheet metal, box steel or a detailed drawing, we cut, bend, weld, finish and powder coat within the same workshop, so parts don't have to travel between sites and quality stays controlled at every stage.

METAL MACHINING

  • CNC / plasma cutting from DXF, DWG files or hand-drawn sketches. Clean cut lines with minimal burr, for single parts or production batches.

  • Sheet bending, hole punching and die stamping. Dimensions are checked against the drawing before moving to the next stage.

  • Welding machine frames, supports, steel structures and stainless parts. Welds are ground and cleaned before surface treatment.

  • Machining machine parts, bushings, shafts, flanges and replacement parts from samples or drawings.

  • We accept 2D/3D drawings, advise on materials and propose a cost-effective machining plan before quoting.

POWDER COATING

  • Degreasing, derusting and surface cleaning — the step that determines how well the coating will adhere.

  • A phosphate layer that resists corrosion and improves adhesion between the metal and the powder coat.

  • Electrostatic powder spraying, evenly covering edges and inner surfaces with no sagging or blotching.

  • Oven-cured so the powder melts and bonds firmly, giving a hard coat that resists impact and scratching better than liquid paint.

  • Coated to RAL colour charts or a sample supplied by the customer; glossy, matte, textured or wrinkle finishes on request.

ADDITIONAL SERVICES

  • We review dimensions and recommend cost-effective materials and machining plans before the order is confirmed.

  • Parts are assembled into complete units after coating, ready to install as soon as they arrive.

  • Coating is wrapped to prevent scratches, and crates are labelled by quantity and marking as the customer requires.

  • Delivery within Binh Duong, Ho Chi Minh City and Dong Nai on a schedule agreed with the customer.

  • We strip old coating, treat the surface and reapply a fresh finish on used frames, cabinets and parts.
